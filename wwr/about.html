Wild River Adventures

Wild River Adventures exists to turn the raw power of the river into unforgettable moments of courage, connection, and joy. We are here to make the outdoors accessible, exciting, and safe for everyone — to help people step outside their comfort zone and discover what they're capable of when the current pushes back.

History

Founded in 2015 by three friends with two rafts and a big dream, Wild River Adventures has grown from a weekend hobby into one of the Cauca River's most trusted adventure companies. Over the years, we've expanded from rafting to hiking, canopy tours, and eco-camping, welcomed thousands of guests from around the world, and built a team of certified guides united by one belief: that the wildest waters teach the most important lessons.

The river keeps flowing — and so does our story. To become the most trusted and beloved adventure company on the Cauca River — a place where first-timers and seasoned rafters alike feel at home, and where every trip leaves the river better than we found it.
