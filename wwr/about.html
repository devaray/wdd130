Rafting Company Title

Wild River Adventures exists to turn the raw power of the river into unforgettable moments of courage, connection, and joy. We are here to make the outdoors accessible, exciting, and safe for everyone — to help people step outside their comfort zone and discover what they're capable of when the current pushes back.

History

Founded in 2015 by three friends with two rafts and a big dream, Wild River Adventures has grown from a weekend hobby into one of the Cauca River's most trusted adventure companies. Over the years, we've expanded from rafting to hiking, canopy tours, and eco-camping, welcomed thousands of guests from around the world, and built a team of certified guides united by one belief: that the wildest waters teach the most important lessons. The river keeps flowing — and so does our story.

Adventure Awaits You!

Wild River Adventures was founded in 2015 by three lifelong friends who grew up along the banks of the Cauca River. What began as a weekend hobby — two secondhand rafts, a borrowed trailer, and a hand-painted sign — quickly grew as word spread among local adventurers and travelers from neighboring towns. By the end of that first season, the three friends knew they had found something worth building. In the years that followed, the company hired its first certified guides, opened a base camp on the river's edge, and expanded beyond rafting into hiking, canopy tours, and eco-camping. After a quiet pandemic season used for training and upgrades, Wild River Adventures returned stronger than ever and has since welcomed thousands of guests from Colombia and around the world — all united by one belief: that the wildest waters teach the most important lessons.
