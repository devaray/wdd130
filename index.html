Ecuador

Ecuador is a small but incredibly diverse country in northwestern South America. It is home to the Amazon rainforest, the Andes Mountains, and the Galápagos Islands, making it one of the most biodiverse places on Earth. The country is known for its rich indigenous cultures and cuisine. Ecuador takes its name from the equator, which runs directly through it.
